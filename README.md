## Projects

**Navigation and GNSS**
- [gps-signal-simulator](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/gps-signal-simulator) — GPS simulator in MATLAB/Simulink: TLE orbits → broadcast ephemeris → IS-GPS-200 navigation message with parity. Work in progress.
- [gnss-positioning-labs](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/gnss-positioning-labs) — Satellite orbits from almanacs, ground tracks, sky plots, DOP and least-squares positioning from real pseudoranges (MATLAB).

**Air traffic management**
- [atfm-ground-holding](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/atfm-ground-holding) — ATFM regulation at Munich: Ground Delay Program vs Ground Holding Problem (integer programming), with emissions and an intermodal scenario (MATLAB).
- [airspace-sectorisation-voronoi](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/airspace-sectorisation-voronoi) — Airspace sectorisation with Voronoi diagrams and genetic algorithms, including multi-objective optimisation (MATLAB).
- [flight-deconflicting-tool](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/flight-deconflicting-tool) — Flight simulator with conflict detection and automatic resolution (C#, WinForms, SQLite).

**CFD**
- [potential-flow-solver](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/potential-flow-solver) — Stream-function solver with SOR for fixed and rotating cylinders, validated against the analytical solution (Python).
- [CFD-Learning](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/CFD-Learning) — Step-by-step CFD fundamentals in Jupyter notebooks: convection, diffusion, Burgers' equation.

**Electronics and digital design**
- [accelerometer-tilt-system](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/accelerometer-tilt-system) — Tilt monitoring with an ADXL335: analog conditioning, comparator and Arduino firmware with PWM motor control (C++, Proteus).
- [harvard-cpu](https://github.com/Andreu-Bueno/harvard-cpu) — 4-bit Harvard-architecture CPU and RAM built from NAND gates in Digital Logic Sim.
